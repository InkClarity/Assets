## Results That Transformed the Business

The rebrand delivered exceptional results positioning Crossed New York as a legitimate premium player in streetwear:

• **41% increase in premium tier purchases** within six months of launch, proving customers were willing to pay more for the elevated brand experience

• **3x improvement in social media engagement** as the new visual identity resonated with their target audience and created shareable moments

• **68% increase in average order value** driven by stronger brand perception and premium positioning combined with successful expansion into premium retail partnerships

• **92% customer satisfaction score** for packaging and unboxing experience, creating loyal brand advocates
