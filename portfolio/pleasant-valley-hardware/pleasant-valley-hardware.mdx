## Our Solution

We partnered with Pleasant Valley Hardware to create a comprehensive brand overhaul bridging community legacy with modern market demands. We created a hand-drawn logo, cohesive brand system, and purposeful design elements.

## Results Delivered

The rebrand delivered transformative business outcomes exceeding all client expectations:

• **35% increase in customer retention** through custom designed loyalty program that rewards repeat business and enhanced brand recognition throughout Pleasant Valley

• **Streamlined operations and stronger competitive positioning** with cohesive branding across all materials, leveraging local expertise with a modern, professional image against big-box retailers

• **Improved customer confidence** both online and in-store, leading to higher conversion rates and repeat business
